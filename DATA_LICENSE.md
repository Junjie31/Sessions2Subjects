# Data License

## `data/paired_scores.csv`

The file `data/paired_scores.csv` contains de-identified (pseudonymized) paired
waveform-agreement scores (MACC) for the six directed cross-domain comparisons
reported in the paper.

- It contains **no video, no raw waveforms, and no directly identifying
  information**. The `subject_id` and `session_id` columns are pseudonymized
  dataset identifiers retained only to preserve the within-subject clustering
  structure that the statistical analysis depends on.
- The scores are derived from three public benchmarks: **PURE**, **UBFC-rPPG**,
  and **mini-MMPD**. The original benchmark recordings are **not** distributed
  with this repository; obtain them from the respective publishers under their
  own access conditions.
- The paired score records are released under the
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
